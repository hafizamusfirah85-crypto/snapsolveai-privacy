Delete Your Account

How to permanently delete your SnapSolve AI account and data.

1

Email us from the address on your account

Send a deletion request from the same email address you used to sign up (or the Google account you used to sign in), so we can confirm it's really you.

✉️ fahadulrahmanyasir@gmail.com
2

Tell us it's a deletion request

A short message is enough — for example: "Please delete my SnapSolve AI account and all associated data." Include the email address your account is registered under if it's different from your sending address.

3

We confirm once it's done

We'll reply to confirm your account and data have been deleted. This is currently handled manually, so please allow a few days.

Typical turnaround: within 7 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account profile and sign-in credentials
  • Your scan history and uploaded homework photos
  • Your daily scan quota and usage records

What may be kept

  • Records we're required to retain for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes, for a limited additional period

For more on how we handle your data, see our Privacy Policy.
