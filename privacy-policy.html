Overview

SnapSolve AI ("the app", "we", "us") helps students scan homework and questions to get AI-generated answers and explanations, across subjects like math, physics, chemistry, and more. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and how it's handled.

By creating an account or using the app, you agree to the practices described here. If you don't agree, please don't use the app.

Information we collect

Account information

To use the app you create an account with an email address and password, or sign in with your Google account. If you use Google Sign-In, we receive the name, email address, and profile photo associated with that account.

Homework photos and scans

When you scan a question, the photo you capture or upload is sent to our server so it can be analyzed and answered, and a copy is stored in your account so you can view your scan history later. Chat messages you send to the AI Chat feature are handled the same way.

Usage and quota data

We keep a record of how many scans you've used each day, tied to your account, so your daily free-scan limit works correctly even if you reinstall the app.

Device and security signals

The app uses Google Play Integrity to confirm requests are coming from a genuine, unmodified copy of the app running on a genuine device. This produces a signed attestation token; it does not identify you personally.

Advertising data

The app is built to show ads through Google AdMob. When ads are active, AdMob may collect an advertising identifier and ad-interaction data to show and measure ads, as described in Google's own privacy policy.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account, and let you sign back in on any device
  • To analyze your scans and chat messages and return an answer
  • To keep your scan history available to you
  • To enforce daily free-scan limits fairly
  • To detect abuse, fraud, and requests that don't come from the real app
  • To send you account-related emails — verifying your email address and resetting your password
  • To show and measure ads, once ads are enabled

Services we rely on

We don't build our own servers for most of this — we rely on established providers, each handling a specific job:

Google Firebase
Account sign-in, storing your profile and scan history, and daily quota tracking.
Google Gemini AI
Analyzes the photos and questions you submit and generates the answers and explanations you see. Requests are routed through our own server, which does not store your Gemini API usage separately from your scan history above.
Google Play Integrity
Confirms the app instance making a request is genuine, to keep the service secure for everyone.
Resend
Delivers the verification and password-reset emails the app sends you.
Google AdMob
Shows in-app advertising, once enabled, and measures ad performance.

Each of these providers processes data under its own privacy policy in addition to this one.

Storage and retention

Your account details, scan history, and usage data are kept for as long as your account is active, so your history and quota stay consistent across reinstalls and devices. If you delete your account, this data is removed from our systems within a reasonable period, except where we're required to keep it for legal or security reasons.

Children's use of this app

SnapSolve AI is a homework-help tool and may be used by students, including those under 13, generally under the guidance of a parent, guardian, or teacher. We don't knowingly collect more information from a child than is needed to provide the app's core features described above, and we don't use children's information for targeted advertising.

If you're a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with information beyond what's needed to use the app, please contact us using the details below and we'll address it.

Security

Your data is stored using Google Firebase's security infrastructure, with access rules that restrict each account to its own data. Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable, industry-standard steps to protect your information.

Your choices

  • Delete your account: available from Settings inside the app, which removes your profile, scan history, and quota data.
  • Reset your password: available from the sign-in screen at any time.
  • Camera and photo access: controlled from your device's app permission settings; the app will simply be unable to scan without it.
  • Advertising identifier: can be reset or limited from your Android device's ad settings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes — for example, when a new feature starts using a new type of data. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
